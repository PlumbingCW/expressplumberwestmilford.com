Why pipes burst in the Highlands

West Milford's long, cold winters put pipe at risk. Water expands when it freezes, and the ice plug downstream pushes pressure back until the pipe wall splits, often not where the ice is but in the section between the plug and the closed faucet.

Homes closed for the winter, lake cottages and unheated crawl spaces and garages are especially exposed. Pipes along exterior walls and in unheated corners are the usual places to look.

Thawing versus repairing

If a pipe is frozen but not yet split, careful thawing with warm air can save it. Open flames never belong near pipe, because fire and soldered joints do not mix, and a thawed pipe may still hide a crack that only shows up when water moves again.

When the pipe is split, the plumber cuts out the damage and replaces the section. If several runs are weak, an expanded repair or repiping is worth discussing.

Burst pipe questions

Do you handle burst pipe repair emergencies at night or on weekends in West Milford?

Yes. A burst pipe is the kind of call the 24/7 line exists for, nights, weekends and holidays included. Shut off the main valve and call.

Where is my main water shutoff?

Usually in the basement near the front wall or where the line enters the house. On well systems it may be near the pressure tank.

Should I turn off the water heater?

Yes if the lines are drained, because a heater with no water coming in can overheat. Switch off the power or gas.

Does insurance cover a burst pipe?

Often it covers the water damage but not the pipe. Call your insurer and ask.

Can I fix it with a clamp?

A clamp is only a temporary stop. The plumber replaces the damaged section.

How do I prevent frozen pipes?

Insulate exposed lines, seal drafts and keep heat on. Let a faucet drip in a hard freeze.

What standard applies to the replacement pipe?

The new section is installed to the National Standard Plumbing Code that New Jersey adopts in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and then pressure tested before the water is turned back on.
