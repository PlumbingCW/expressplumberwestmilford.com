If the disposal hums but does not spin, turn off the switch and do not put your hand in. If it is leaking under the sink, turn the power off at the switch or breaker and keep the cabinet empty of anything that can soak.

A jam is usually a spoon, a bone or a fibrous scrap that wedged against the flywheel. A leak is most often at a seal, the sink flange or the drain connection, and a unit that leaks from its own body is usually finished.

Before anything else, ask whether you have a septic system. Many West Milford homes do, and a grinder adds solids to a tank that already has limits.

Septic and disposals do not always mix

The Kinnelon Board of Health, across the county line, tells residents on septic not to add a garbage grinder to an existing system. The reasoning applies to West Milford too, since the township's own study found that about 72 percent of local soils have severe septic limitations.

If your house is on septic, a disposal is a trade-off. The plumber can repair or replace one if you want it, and also explain how it affects pumping. Compost scraps are the easier answer.

Emergency check: diagnose by sound and symptom

The way a disposal fails tells you what is wrong.

Humming, no spin

A jam. Power off, then free the flywheel from below with a hex key or from above with a wooden spoon handle.

Nothing at all

A tripped reset button on the bottom or a bad switch or outlet.

Water under the unit

A leak at the sink flange, the drain connection or the housing.

Sink drains slowly with the unit on

A partial clog in the trap or the line beyond the unit.

Repair or replace

A jam or a loose connection is a repair. A unit that leaks from the housing, has rusted through or trips the reset every few uses is at the end of its life.

If the sink backs up when the disposal runs, the problem may be in the drain line rather than the unit, and drain cleaning solves it.

Garbage disposal questions

Do you handle garbage disposal repair emergencies at night or on weekends in West Milford?

Yes. A leaking or jammed disposal is handled on the 24/7 line at any hour. Turn off the power to the unit and call.

Why does my disposal hum?

The motor is trying to turn but the flywheel is stuck. Turn it off and call, or free it with the manual hex key.

Is it safe to put my hand in a disposal?

Never. Even with power off, treat it as a hazard. Use tongs or a spoon handle.

Can I install a disposal on a septic system?

It can be done, but it adds solids to the tank. Ask the plumber before you decide.

How long does a disposal last?

Many last several years, depending on use and what goes in. A leaking housing means replacement.

What if the sink is clogged but the disposal runs?

The blockage is likely in the trap or the line below. The plumber clears it.

What standard covers a disposal installation?

The drain connection follows the National Standard Plumbing Code as adopted in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and the electrical side follows the National Electrical Code (NEC), so a licensed electrician may be needed for a new outlet.
