If a faucet is spraying or a supply line under the sink is leaking, close the small stop valves under the sink by turning them clockwise. If there are no stop valves or they will not turn, shut off the main water valve and call our 24/7 line.

A faucet that drips is a smaller problem, but it is a steady one. A cartridge, washer or seat that has worn out lets water pass, and the drip tends to get worse as the metal beneath erodes.

Most faucet repairs are about identifying the brand and style, then replacing the cartridge, seat or the whole fixture when the parts cost more than a new faucet.

Emergency or not? Why faucets fail

Faucets are used dozens of times a day, so their moving parts wear steadily. Mineral buildup from hard water speeds this up and can lock the handle in place.

Many West Milford homes use private wells, and the township's groundwater study notes that wells draw on bedrock aquifers. Water from bedrock often carries minerals that crust aerators and cartridges, so a yearly cleaning of aerators saves repairs.

Kitchen faucets and handles

Kitchen faucets with pull-down sprayers add hoses, weights and quick connectors that can fail. A handle that loosens or a spout that wobbles often just needs the retaining parts tightened or replaced.

If the sink area also has a disposal, the repair visit is a good moment to check that connection through garbage disposal repair.

Check these before you call

Make sure the shutoff valves under the sink are fully open, clean the aerator by unscrewing it and soaking it in vinegar and look for a bent supply line. These steps solve some low-flow calls without a visit, and the plumber will not mind if you tried them.

Bathroom and laundry faucets

Bathroom sinks often have pop-up drains and small supply lines that leak at the connections. Laundry sinks and utility faucets may have hose threads that crack or valves that seize after years of sitting open.

These are quick visits when the parts are on hand, and the plumber replaces tired supply lines at the same time, since a new faucet on a brittle line is a short-lived repair.

Faucet repair questions

Do you handle faucet repair emergencies at night or on weekends in West Milford?

Yes. A spraying or leaking faucet is handled on the 24/7 line at any hour. Close the stop valve under the sink or the main water valve and call.

Why does my faucet drip even when closed?

A worn cartridge, washer or seat is the usual cause. The plumber replaces the part that is worn.

Can you repair a single-handle kitchen faucet?

Yes. Most single-handle faucets use a cartridge that can be replaced.

What if my shutoff valves will not turn?

Old valves can seize. The plumber can replace them so future repairs are easier.

Why is the water pressure low from one faucet?

A clogged aerator or a partly closed stop valve is common. Hard water deposits are a frequent cause.

Do you install customer-supplied faucets?

Yes, as long as the fixture and parts are in good order.

Are faucet repairs regulated?

Simple repairs rarely need a permit, but all plumbing work in New Jersey must be done by licensed professionals, and new piping follows the National Standard Plumbing Code under N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.
