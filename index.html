What makes plumbing in West Milford different

The township's own groundwater study says groundwater is the primary drinking water source, and it recorded 2,449 domestic wells. A house on a well has a pump, a pressure tank and a switch that a house on city water does not, so a plumbing call often includes questions about pressure and pump cycling, which is where leak detection often starts.

The same study found that about 72 percent of local soils have severe limitations for conventional septic systems. That is why so many drain calls here begin with a question about the septic tank, and why the plumber tells you when a problem is beyond the house line.

Planned work as well as emergencies

Not every call is urgent. Homeowners also book a new heater through water heater installation, a camera look through sewer camera inspection before buying or new pipe through repiping.

For business owners, commercial plumbing covers shops, offices and kitchens where a plumbing failure stops the day.

Licensed plumbers, clear prices

New Jersey allows plumbing work only by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees, and the state runs a License Verification System through the Division of Consumer Affairs. You can check any plumber there before you hire.

We give a clear price before work starts, and the work follows the National Standard Plumbing Code that New Jersey adopts in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. Insurance can help with sudden water damage in many policies, so ask your carrier early, and call us for your real number.

What should I do before the plumber arrives?
