Newfoundland is a community along Route 23 in northern New Jersey, and it is split between two municipalities. West Milford Township in Passaic County holds part of it, and Jefferson Township in Morris County holds the rest.

We cover the West Milford side. If your house is across the line in Jefferson, tell us when you call, because permits and rules there come from a different municipality.

Newfoundland in brief

Newfoundland is an unincorporated community and census-designated place at about 761 feet in elevation, with ZIP code 07435. The Pequannock River divides it between Passaic and Morris Counties, and it follows the New York, Susquehanna and Western freight line.

The 2020 Census counted 1,145 people in the census-designated place, which covers more than the West Milford portion alone.

Why the township line matters

A plumbing job here can fall under two different building departments depending on which side of the line the house sits. On the West Milford side, permit questions go to the township, and permit rules vary, so ask the West Milford building department at 1480 Union Valley Road. New Jersey adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code through N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.

Mail can hide the line. A 07435 address does not tell you which municipality you are in. Your tax bill or property record shows it, and it is worth checking before you book a permitted job.

A higher, colder location

Newfoundland sits higher than Hewitt, about 761 feet against 430 feet. Higher ground generally means colder nights in winter, so the usual cold-weather advice applies. Know where your main shutoff is, insulate pipes in unheated spaces and let a faucet drip during a hard freeze.

If a pipe does freeze and split, shut off the water and call. A burst pipe repair visit finds the split, replaces the section and pressure-tests the line before the walls are closed.

Drains near the Pequannock River

The river and the surrounding Newark watershed land shape the area, and Newark owns a large part of the Pequannock River Watershed. That is a fact about land ownership. It does not tell us anything about a given house drain.

When a drain backs up, the plumber asks whether the house is on public sewer or a septic system, then clears the line with drain cleaning. If the same drain keeps returning, a camera look through sewer camera inspection shows what is in the line.

When a drain backs up

Do these four things before the plumber arrives.

Stop using water

Do not flush or run taps. Water has nowhere to go and will come back up another drain.

Look at the lowest drain

If a basement floor drain or tub is bubbling, the blockage is likely in the main line.

Do not pour chemicals

Drain chemicals can harm pipes and are unsafe for the plumber to work around.

Note the history

Tell the plumber how long it has been slow and whether it is one fixture or several.

Resort history, modern houses

Newfoundland was a resort destination around 1900, when hotels such as Brown's Hotel and the Idylease Inn depended on the railway. The station was built in 1872 and appears in the 2003 film The Station Agent.

That history is local color, and we do not assume it says anything about the pipes in a house today. The plumber looks at what is there.

Newfoundland plumbing questions

Which part of Newfoundland do you cover?

We cover the West Milford side in Passaic County. Jefferson Township in Morris County is a separate municipality, so tell us when you call if you are not sure.

What ZIP code is Newfoundland?

Newfoundland uses ZIP code 07435. That ZIP code serves both the West Milford and Jefferson Township portions.

Who issues permits on the West Milford side?

Permit rules vary, so ask the West Milford building department. It is at 1480 Union Valley Road.

My pipes froze. What do I do first?

Shut off the main valve, open a faucet and call. Do not use an open flame to thaw a pipe.

How quickly can someone come?

We offer same-day appointments when available. The plumber confirms timing when you call.
