West Milford Express Plumbing takes emergency and planned plumbing calls for the township of West Milford and the nearby towns of Ringwood, Bloomingdale and Kinnelon. Our address is 1467 Union Valley Rd, West Milford, NJ 07480, and the phone line is open 24 hours a day.

We keep the business simple. You call one number, describe the problem and hear what to do next. A licensed plumber then comes out, finds the cause and gives you a clear price before the work starts.

How a call works

The dispatcher asks where the water is, what you have shut off and which kind of system you have, such as public water, a private well, sewer or septic. Those answers shape the first visit.

Appointments are the same day when the schedule allows. We do not promise an arrival time over the phone, because weather, distance and other emergencies change what is possible, and we would rather tell you the truth than a number.

How we price

The price depends on what the plumber finds, so we do not quote exact numbers without seeing the problem. What we do promise is that you hear a clear price before work starts and can say no.

Each service page lists the factors that move the price, including access, pipe condition, parts and the time of the call.

Licensing in New Jersey

New Jersey allows plumbing work only by a master plumber, an authorized plumbing contractor or their employees. The Division of Consumer Affairs runs a License Verification System, and you can reach its hotline at (973) 504-6200 or (800) 242-5846.

We encourage every customer to verify a plumber before work begins, and you are welcome to ask for licensing details when you call.

Service area

We serve all of West Milford and the neighboring boroughs. The areas page lists each town with local notes, and the services page lists everything we handle.

What we handle

Emergency calls make up most of the work, including backed-up drains, failed water heaters, leaking and burst pipes, sump pump failures and sewer backups. Planned work includes water heater installation, camera inspections, repiping and plumbing for remodels and additions.

Each of these has its own page with the signs to look for, what to do while you wait and the factors that move the price. We wrote those pages for the way people actually search when something goes wrong.

What we will not claim

You will not find invented reviews, made-up customer counts or awards on this site. We would rather show you plain information than numbers we cannot back up.

We also do not promise arrival times. The line is open around the clock and appointments are the same day when the schedule allows, and that is the promise we can keep.

Water, wells and septic in the township

Plumbing in West Milford depends on where your water and waste go. The township's groundwater study says groundwater is the primary drinking water source and recorded 2,449 domestic wells, which means many houses have a pump and a pressure tank to look after.

The same study found that about 72 percent of local soils have severe limitations for conventional septic systems. That shapes how we approach drains, disposals and sewer work, and we tell you when a problem is outside the house line.

Before you hire any plumber

Ask for a clear price in writing before work starts, ask who will do the work and check their license through the state system. Ask whether a permit is needed, and ask what the warranty covers.

Permit rules vary, so ask the West Milford building department about your project, or the borough office if you are in Ringwood, Bloomingdale or Kinnelon. We are glad to answer the same questions about our own work when you call.

Plumbing work in New Jersey follows the state plumbing subcode, which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code under N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15.

Safety comes before speed

Some situations are not plumbing calls first. If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility from outside. If water has reached electrical panels or outlets, stay clear and call 911 if anyone is in danger.

Once the immediate danger is handled, call us. We will tell you how to limit the damage while a plumber is on the way, and what to photograph for your insurance claim.

Reaching us by email

Email contact@expressplumberwestmilford.com for non-urgent questions, such as planning a remodel or asking about a past visit. We cannot dispatch from an email, so for anything urgent, call the 24/7 line instead.

Questions before you call?

The line is open any time. Ask the dispatcher anything about how we work.
