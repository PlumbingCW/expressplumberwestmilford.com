If your water heater is leaking, treat it as urgent. Call our 24/7 line, shut off the cold water supply valve on top of the tank, and switch the unit off at the breaker, or turn the gas control to pilot or off, before you do anything else.

A tank that only runs cold is less dramatic but still worth a quick call, especially in winter when a family needs showers and a laundry load before morning. Many of the visits start with a simple cause such as a tripped breaker, a failed igniter or a pilot that blew out, and those fixes are often finished in one trip.

Heaters in West Milford basements work harder than most because the water arrives cold and the houses sit in the Highlands where winters are long. A heater past its prime can fail suddenly, so the plumber also looks at age and condition before recommending a repair.

What each symptom usually means

Gas and electric heaters fail in different ways, so the cards below split the common symptoms by fuel type and by where the water shows up.

Gas heater, no hot water at all

A pilot that went out, a dirty or failed igniter or thermocouple, or a gas control that has shut itself down.

Electric heater, no hot water

A tripped breaker, a failed upper or lower heating element or a bad thermostat.

Hot water that runs out fast

A broken dip tube, a failed lower element or heavy sediment taking up space in the tank.

Water around the base

A leaking drain valve or relief valve is fixable. A leak from the tank itself means replacement.

Emergency repair or replacement: how we decide

A tank usually earns a repair when the problem is a part and the tank is sound. It points toward replacement when the shell leaks, the unit is well past the age most tanks reach or repairs are stacking up.

Why West Milford tanks collect sediment

Many West Milford homes draw from private wells, and the township's groundwater study recorded 2,449 domestic wells. Well water carries dissolved minerals and sometimes sand or fine sediment, and that material settles on the bottom of a tank where it insulates the burner or element and shortens the heater's life.

A yearly flush and a look at the anode rod slow that down. If your water leaves white scale on faucets or fixtures, tell the plumber, because it changes how often the tank should be maintained.

Water heater repair questions answered

Do you handle water heater repair emergencies at night or on weekends in West Milford?

Yes. A leaking or dead water heater is treated as an emergency, and the 24/7 line is open nights, weekends and holidays. Shut off the cold water at the tank and the power or gas, then call.

How do I shut off a leaking water heater?

Turn the cold water supply valve on top of the tank clockwise to close it. Then switch off the breaker for an electric unit, or turn a gas unit's control to the off or pilot setting.

Why does my gas water heater keep going out?

A weak thermocouple, a dirty pilot assembly or a draft problem are common causes. The plumber checks all three and tests the flame before leaving.

Is a rumbling tank dangerous?

Rumbling usually means sediment boiling beneath the burner. It is not an emergency, but it hurts efficiency and shortens the tank's life, so schedule a flush.

Can you repair the heater if it is old?

Often yes, if the tank itself is sound. We tell you plainly when a repair is only buying a short stretch of time.

What if the relief valve is dripping?

A dripping relief valve can mean excess pressure or temperature or a worn valve. Do not cap it. Call so the cause is found and the valve is replaced safely.

Which standards apply to a water heater repair?

Repairs to gas, venting and relief piping follow the state plumbing subcode, which adopts the National Standard Plumbing Code under N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15, and manufacturer instructions apply to the appliance itself.
