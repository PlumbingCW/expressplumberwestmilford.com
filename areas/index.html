Home township

West Milford, Passaic County

West Milford is the largest municipality in Passaic County by area at about 76 square miles, incorporated in 1834. It includes the communities of Hewitt (ZIP 07421), Newfoundland (07435), Oak Ridge (07438), Upper Greenwood Lake, Macopin and Apshawa, and the main township ZIP is 07480.

Routes 23, County Road 511 and County Road 513 connect these communities, and the distances are long. When you call, give the nearest cross street and any lake-association gate so the plumber can plan the route.
