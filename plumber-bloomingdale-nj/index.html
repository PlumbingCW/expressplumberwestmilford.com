Soft water, mild acidity and older pipes

Bloomingdale Water Department buys its water in bulk from the Butler Water Department, which draws on the Kakeout Reservoir. The borough's published results list hardness around 36 parts per million, which is soft, and a pH of about 6.86, which is just under neutral.

Soft, slightly acidic water scales fixtures much less than hard well water does, but over many years it can be mildly aggressive toward copper. If you have older copper lines, pinhole leaks are one thing to watch, and leak detection finds them before a wall is opened.

Bloomingdale at a glance

The borough sits in Passaic County along the Pequannock River and borders Pompton Lakes, Ringwood, Wanaque, West Milford, Butler and Riverdale. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for ZIP 07403 and the lake communities of Glen Wild Lake, Lake Iosco, Kampfe Lake and the Morse Lakes.

County Road 511 runs through town and the Paterson-Hamburg Turnpike holds the main business district, so a commercial call and a residential call can come from the same street.

Before We Arrive: burst pipe or active leak

Whether it is a cottage near Lake Iosco or a house near Mayor Dunleavy Memorial Park, these steps limit the damage.

Stop the water

Turn off the main valve. Know where it is before you need it.

Protect the electrics

Leave the breaker alone if the panel is wet, and keep cords and appliances out of standing water.

Make space

Move furniture and boxes, then open a low faucet to drain the lines.

Phone the line

Call the 24/7 number and give your address and what you have already shut off.

Repiping and leaks in older cottages

Many of the smaller houses near the lakes were built as summer cottages and have thin, old lines. Repeated leaks in different spots are the sign that a section of the house, or the whole house, is ready for repiping.

The plumber looks at the pipe in the open basement first, then at the lines behind finished walls, and gives a clear price before work starts.
