What to tell the dispatcher

Give the address and the nearest cross street, then describe what is happening and what you have already done, such as shutting off the main valve. Mention whether you have a well or public water and sewer or septic.

If there is a gate, a lake-association entrance or a long driveway, say so. It helps the plumber find you quickly.
