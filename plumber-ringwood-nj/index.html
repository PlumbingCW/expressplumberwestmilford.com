Ringwood in brief

Ringwood sits in the Highlands of northern Passaic County, borders West Milford and is crossed by County Road 511, known locally as Greenwood Lake Turnpike, and County Road 692, Skyline Drive. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock for every part of the borough, including the private lake communities of Cupsaw Lake, Erskine Lakes and Skyline Lakes.

Ringwood State Park, with Ringwood Manor, Skylands Manor and Shepherd Lake, takes up a large part of the borough, so many roads run along the park edge and some homes are quite far apart. When you call, give the cross street and any gate or lake-association entrance the driver needs.

Hard water and what it does to a water heater

The Borough of Ringwood's 2024 water quality report for its municipal system lists hardness up to 191 parts per million, with a reported range from about 38 to 191. About 80 percent of that water comes from four borough wells and the rest is purchased from the Passaic Valley Water Commission, which draws on the Wanaque Reservoir.

Hardness at that level leaves scale on heating elements and in the bottom of tanks, so a water heater here benefits from a yearly flush and an anode rod check. If your heater is cold or rumbling, our water heater repair visit starts with sediment and scale before anything more expensive.

Before We Arrive: burst pipe or active leak in Ringwood

Ringwood winters are long and the older cottages around the lakes often have pipes in crawl spaces and exterior walls. These four steps keep the damage small while the plumber is on the way.

Shut off the main

Close the main water valve. If you are on a private well, switch off the pump breaker as well.

Kill power to wet areas

Only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water. Otherwise wait for the plumber.

Clear the area

Move rugs, boxes and electronics away from the water and open a faucet low in the house to relieve pressure.

Call our 24/7 line

Drain and sewer problems in lake-area houses

Many Ringwood houses began as seasonal homes and were later insulated and converted for year-round use. Drain lines in those cottages are often small, old and routed in odd ways, and they clog sooner when a full household uses them every day.

If a kitchen or basement drain backs up, the emergency drain cleaning visit begins at the nearest cleanout. When a line keeps clogging, the plumber recommends a camera look so the real cause is found.

Does hard water in Ringwood affect my water heater?
