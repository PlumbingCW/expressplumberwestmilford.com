Terms of Use

The basic terms for using this website and booking service by phone.

The information on this website is general and meant to help you understand common plumbing problems. It is not a substitute for an inspection by a licensed plumber.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

Service by phone

Services are arranged by telephone. The plumber gives a clear price before work starts, and work begins only after you agree to it. Prices depend on the conditions found on site.

Appointment times are estimates and may change with weather, distance and other emergencies.

Safety

If you smell gas, leave the building and call your gas utility from outside. If you have an immediate danger to life, call 911 first.

Changes

We may update this website and these terms from time to time. The date of the latest review is November 16, 2026.
