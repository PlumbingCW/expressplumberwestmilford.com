Overview

Plumbing problems in the Highlands tend to cluster around a few themes: water that arrives from wells or small systems, septic and sewer lines on rocky ground, long cold winters and houses of many ages. Our services follow those themes.

Emergency services run on the 24/7 line, so a plumber can respond to a backup, a leak or a failed heater at any hour, and every job follows the National Standard Plumbing Code that New Jersey adopts in N.J.A.C. 5:23-3.15. Planned services are scheduled by phone, so you can talk through the scope and the price before any work is booked.
