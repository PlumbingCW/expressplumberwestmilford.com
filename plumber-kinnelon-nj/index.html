Kinnelon is a Morris County borough, so permits and health rules come from Kinnelon, not from West Milford. The plumber knows that before the call is over and plans accordingly.

If a drain is backing up, a toilet is overflowing or a pipe has burst, shut off the water and call our 24/7 line. Kinnelon is about twelve miles from our base by road, and the line works the same at night as at noon.

The Kinnelon Board of Health says most residents have a septic system, so many drain calls here begin with a question about the tank.

Septic first, then plumbing

The borough's Board of Health recommends pumping a septic tank every three years, and every two as the system ages, and tells residents not to add a garbage grinder to an existing system. A Septic Repair Application is available through the borough.

When a slow drain turns out to be a full tank or a clogged outlet, cabling the house line does nothing. Our drain cleaning visit checks the house side, and the plumber says plainly when the problem is at the tank.

Kinnelon in brief

Kinnelon runs along Route 23 on its northern edge and includes the gated Smoke Rise community, built from 1946 with about 900 homes around Lake Kinnelon and Kitty Ann Mountain, and the denser Fayson Lakes area that began as a summer colony in 1925. Dispatch for the whole borough is available around the clock.

The USPS lists ZIP 07405 under the city name Butler, so mail for Kinnelon addresses may show Butler even though the house is in Kinnelon. Silas Condict County Park, the Splitrock Reservoir and watershed lands add large wooded areas and some long driveways.

Public water and what it means for pipes

Kinnelon's water department gets its supply from the Butler Water Company, drawing on the Kakeout Reservoir, and the borough reports no known lead service lines. Hardness is not reported in the data we have, so ask the department for the latest report if scale is a concern at your address.

Whatever the water, an older home benefits from a look at the supply pipe through sewer camera inspection on the drain side and a leak check on the supply side.

Why does my Kinnelon address say Butler?

Is my house on septic?

Can you pump a septic tank?
